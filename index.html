Frequently asked questions

Learn how Vybemuse works.

How do I search for music?

Choose Search music, enter a song, artist or genre, and select a music platform to view its results.

How do I retrieve video information?

Choose Paste video URL, enter a supported HTTPS link and select Find video information.

How do I prepare a download?

Review the information, choose an available format, confirm your permission and select Prepare my download.

Can I download any video?

No. Only use media you own, have licensed, or have explicit permission to download. Respect applicable platform terms and copyright rules.

Does Vybemuse host music?

Vybemuse helps users discover music and access supported media information. Search results on third-party platforms remain subject to those platforms' rules.

About Vybemuse

Vybemuse is a music discovery platform designed to make it easier to explore African music, Rwandan artists, popular genres and international sounds. Users can search popular music platforms or retrieve information about supported media links.

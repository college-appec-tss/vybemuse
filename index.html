Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about using Vybemuse.

How do I search for music?

Select Discover music, enter a song, artist or genre, and choose a platform to explore the results.

How do I retrieve video information?

Select Video URL, paste a supported HTTPS link and click Find video information.

How do I prepare a download?

Review the information, choose an available format, confirm your permission and select Prepare my download.

Can I download any video?

No. Only use media you own, have licensed or have permission to download. Respect copyright and platform rules.

Does Vybemuse host music?

Vybemuse helps users discover music and access supported media information. Third-party platforms retain control of their content.

Is Vybemuse mobile-friendly?

Yes. The interface adjusts to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

About Vybemuse

Vybemuse is a music discovery platform designed to make it easier to explore African music, Rwandan artists, popular genres and international sounds. Users can search popular music platforms or retrieve information about supported media links. Our goal is to make music discovery straightforward, accessible and enjoyable.
